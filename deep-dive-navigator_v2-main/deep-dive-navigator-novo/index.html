    Before you start

    Please don't share personal information

    • The text of the PDF you choose, and the sentences you listen to, are sent to external AI services (Google Gemini, possibly through OpenRouter) to build your expedition and read it aloud.
    • We have no control over how these companies store or use that text. This may include using it to train their AI models.
    • Your account, progress and the expedition in progress are saved on our servers. We do not keep the PDF file itself.

    Please do not upload documents that contain:

    names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, ID or student numbers, passwords, health information or details about other people — or unpublished work you are not allowed to share.

    Your data

    Privacy notice

    Version · Deep Dive Navigator is a beta prototype built for academic research and study support.

    What we store

    • Your email, username, explorer name and avatar.
    • Your game progress: XP, shards, shop items, sound preferences and weekly XP.
    • Your friend requests and friendships (only usernames are visible to other explorers — never your email).
    • The expedition in progress: the sections, hints and quiz questions generated from your PDF. We do not keep the PDF file itself.
    • When you accepted this notice, and which version.

    Who else handles your data

    • AI service (Google Gemini, possibly through OpenRouter): the text of the PDF you choose is sent to it to build your expedition, and the sentences you listen to are sent to Google's text-to-speech. Each provider applies its own privacy policy. Please do not upload documents with personal, confidential or sensitive information.
    • Supabase: hosts accounts and the database.
    • Netlify: hosts the website. Google Fonts serves the typefaces.

    What we use it for

    Only to run the app: sign you in, save your progress, build your expeditions, and power friends and rankings. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

    How long we keep it

    For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, progress, expedition and friendships are permanently erased.

    Your rights (LGPD)

    You can, at any time and from the ⚙ Account panel: download a copy of your data, and delete your account. You can also withdraw your consent by deleting your account. To correct your data, ask us.
